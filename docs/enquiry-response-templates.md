# Vayucred enquiry response templates

These are operational starting points, not automated messages. Personalise every response, keep claims factual and do not request confidential project evidence over ordinary email.

Thank you for contacting Vayucred. We have received your message about [high-level topic]. We will review the context and respond within two business days with the most useful next step.

For now, please do not email credentials, sensitive personal information, confidential project evidence or commercially sensitive files. If such material becomes necessary, we will first agree an appropriate secure channel.
